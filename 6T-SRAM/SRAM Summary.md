To ensure writability:
$$
\text{Pull-up ratio}= \frac{\left( \frac{W}{L} \right)_{PMOS}}{\left( \frac{W}{L} \right)_{Access}}<1.5
$$

To ensure readability:
$$
\text{Cell Ratio (Beta Ratio)}  =\frac{\left( \frac{W}{L} \right)_{PMOS}}{\left( \frac{W}{L} \right)_{Access}} > 1
$$


*Note*: [[NMOS vs PMOS|PMOS]] current $\propto$ hole mobility $\mu_{h}$, which is lower than electron mobility $\mu_{e}$ - Typically $\mu_{e} \approx 2\mu_{h}$
