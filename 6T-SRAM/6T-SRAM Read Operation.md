- Both bit lines are pre-charged to $V_{DD}$ 
- Word-line is turned on for one of the cells on the $BL$ 
- Cell pulls down either $BL$ or $BLB$ 
- Sense amp regenerates the differential signal 
- Data should not flip after read access 
- Driver transistor must be stronger than access transistor

**How to Speed up Read Operation**:
- *use a sense amp* 

$$
\text{Bitline delay}= \frac{C_{bit-line}\Delta V_{bit-line}}{I_{cell}}
$$
- $C_{bit-line}$ is large to the large number of cells attached 
- $I_{cell}$ is small to to high density cells 
- $\Delta V_{bit-line}$ has to be minimized to high speed
	- $<100$mV has bit-line voltage difference generated by SRAM cell
	- Let the sense amp finish its job
	- Increased noise sensitivity - circuit complexity
