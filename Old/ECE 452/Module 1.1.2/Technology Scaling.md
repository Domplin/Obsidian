- Key enabler for smaller, faster and more power efficient computing systems

- Technology scaling has a threefold objective:
	- Increase the transistor density
	- Reduce the gate delay
	- Reduce the power consumption

- Device dimensions (lateral and vertical) and voltages are reduced by $\frac{1}{\alpha}$ (30% or 0.7x)

- Dennard Scaling (aka MOSFET scaling; 1774)
	- As transistors get smaller, there power density stays constant so that the power use stays in proportion with area
		- because both voltage and current scale (downward) with length

- Technology generation spans 2-3 years
