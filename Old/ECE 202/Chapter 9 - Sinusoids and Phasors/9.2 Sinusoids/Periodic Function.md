A periodic Function is one that satisfies $$f(t) = f(t+nT)$$
for all t and for all integers n

*v(t)* repeats itself every T seconds is shown by replacing t by *t+T* in [[Sinusoidal Voltage]]

$$
v(t+T) = v_{m}\sin \omega(t+T) = V_{m}\omega(t+\frac{2\pi}{\omega})
$$

$$
V_{m}\sin(\omega t+w\pi) = V_{m}\sin \omega t=v(t)
$$
Hence:

$$
v(t+T) = v(t)
$$
