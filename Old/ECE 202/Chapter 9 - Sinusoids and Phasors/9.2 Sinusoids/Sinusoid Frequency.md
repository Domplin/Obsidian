The *period* of the function is the time to complete one cycle or the number of seconds per cycle. The reciprocal of this quantity is the number of cycles per second known as the *cyclic frequency f*:
Eqs. (9.5)
$$
\textit{f} = \frac{1}{T}
$$
from [[Period of sinusoid]] equation 9.2 and equation 9.5:
$$
\omega=2\pi \textit{f}
$$
