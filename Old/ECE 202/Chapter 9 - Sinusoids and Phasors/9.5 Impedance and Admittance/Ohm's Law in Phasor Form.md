
Ohm’s Law in [[Phasor|phasor]] form for any type of element as:
$$
\begin{align}
Z=\frac{V}{I} \\
\text{or} \\
V=ZI 
\end{align}
$$
Where **Z** is a frequency-dependent quantity known as *[[Impedance]]*, measured in ohms.

Given the voltage-current relations for the three passive elements:
$$
\begin{align}
V=RI \\
V=j\omega LI \\
V=\frac{I}{j\omega C}
\end{align}
$$
They can be written in terms of the ratio of the phasor voltage to the phasor current as:
$$
\begin{align}
\frac{V}{I}=R \\
\frac{V}{I}=j\omega L \\
\frac{V}{I}=\frac{1}{j\omega C}
\end{align}
$$
