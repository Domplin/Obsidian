The **Impedance*** of Z of a circuit is the ratio of the phasor voltage, **V**, to the phasor current, **I**, measured in ohms, $\ohm$.

The impedance represents the opposition that the circuit exhibits to the flow of sinusoidal current. 

$$
Z=\frac{V}{I}

$$
or
$$
V=ZI
$$
