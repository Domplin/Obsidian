A **Phasor** is a complex number that represents the amplitude and phase of a sinusoid.
Its a mean of simply analyzing AC circuits using complex numbers.

A complex number can be written in rectangular form as: $z = x +jy$
where $j = \sqrt{ -1 }$ 

*x* is the real part of *z* 
*y* is the imaginary part of *z* 

The complex number *z* can also be written in [[Exponential Form]] or [[Complex polar]]
where *r* is the magnitude of *z* and $\phi$ is the phase of *z*.

*z* can  be written in 3 ways:
$$
\begin{align}
z=x+jy \\
z=r\angle \phi \\
z =re^{j\phi}
\end{align}
$$

For example:
$v(t)=3\cos(\omega t+30^\degree)$ can be rewritten in **phasor form** as
$\bar{V}=\angle 30^\degree$
