Given rectangular form it can be converted to polar form. Shown in $Figure (9.6)$ *x*-axis represents the real part and the *y*-axis represents the imaginary part of a complex number.
 
Given X and Y, $\phi$ and *r* can be found:
$$
\begin{align}
r=\sqrt{ x^2+y^2 } \\
\phi = \tan^{-1}\frac{y}{x}
\end{align}
$$
