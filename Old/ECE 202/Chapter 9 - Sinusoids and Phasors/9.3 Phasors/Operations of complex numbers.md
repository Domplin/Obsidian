Addition and subtraction of complex numbers are better performed in rectangular form.
Multiplication and division is better done in polar form.

Given the complex numbers:
$$
\begin{align}
z=x+jy =r\angle\phi \\
z_{1}=x_{1}+jy_{1}=r_{1}\angle \phi_{1} \\
z_{2}=x_{2}+jy_{2}=r_{2}\angle\phi_{2}
\end{align}
$$

$Addition:$
$$
z_{1}+z_{2} = (x_{1} + x_{2}) +j(y_{1} +y_{2})
$$
$Subtraction:$
$$
z_{1} - z_{2} = (x_{1} -x_{2}) +j(y_{1}- y_{2})
$$
$Multiplication:$
$$
z_{1}z_{2}=r_{1}r_{2}\angle\phi_{1}-\phi_{2}
$$
$Division:$
$$
\frac{z_{1}}{z_{2}}=\frac{r_{1}}{r_{2}}\angle\phi_{1}-\phi_{2}
$$
$Reciprocal:$
$$
\frac{1}{z}=\frac{1}{r}\angle-\phi
$$
$Square root:$
$$
\sqrt{ z }= \sqrt{ r }\angle \frac{\phi}{2}
$$
$Complex Conjugate:$
$$
z^*= x-jy=r\angle-\phi=\mathrm{re}^{-j\phi}
$$
