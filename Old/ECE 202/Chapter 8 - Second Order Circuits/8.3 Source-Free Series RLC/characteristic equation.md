$$
s^2+\frac{R}{L}s+\frac{1}{LC} =0
$$
Is known as the characteristic equation.
The roots of the equation dictate the character of $i$ 
The two roots are:
$$
\begin{align}
s_{1}=-\frac{R}{2L}+\sqrt{ \left( \frac{R}{2L} \right)^2 -\frac{1}{LC} } \\
s_{2}=-\frac{R}{2L}-\sqrt{ \left( \frac{R}{2L} \right)^2 -\frac{1}{LC} }
\end{align}
$$

A more compact way of expressing the roots are:
$$
\begin{align}
s_{1}=-\alpha+\sqrt{ \alpha^2-\omega_{0}^2 } \\
s_{2}=-\alpha+\sqrt{ \alpha^2-\omega_{0}^2 }
\end{align}
$$
where
$$
\begin{align}
\alpha= \frac{R}{2L} \\
\omega_{0}=\frac{1}{\sqrt{ LC }}
\end{align}
$$

The roots of $s_{1}$ and $s_{2}$ are called *natural frequencies* measured in nepers per second (Np/s) 

$\omega_{0}$ is known as the *resonant frequency* or strictly as the *undamped natural frequency* expressed in radians per second (rad/s)
