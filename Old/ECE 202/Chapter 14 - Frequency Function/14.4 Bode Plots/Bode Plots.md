**Bode Plots** are semi-log plots of the magnitude (in decibels) and phase (in degrees) of a transfer function versus frequency. 

Bode plots contain the same information as the non-logarithmic plots, but they are much easier to construct.

The [[Transfer Function]] can be written as:
$$
H=H\angle\phi=He^{j\phi}
$$
Taking the natural log of both sides:
$$
\ln H=\ln H+\ln e^{j\phi}=\ln H +j\phi
$$ 
Thus the real part of $\ln H$ is a function of the magnitude while the imaginary part is the phase. In a Bode magnitude plot, the gain:
$$
H_{dB}=20\log_{10} H
$$

is plotted in decibels (dB) versus frequency.
