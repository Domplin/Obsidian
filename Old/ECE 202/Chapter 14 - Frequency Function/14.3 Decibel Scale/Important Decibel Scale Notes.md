Three important things from the [[Decibel Scale]] equations:

1) That $10\log_{10}$ is used for power while $20\log_{10}$ is used for voltage or current because the square relations between them ($P=\frac{V^2}{R}=I^2R$)
2) That the dB value is a logarithmic measurement of the ratio of one variable to another *of the same type*. Therefore it applies in expressing the [[Transfer Function|transfer function]] **H** 
3) It is important to note that we only use voltage and current magnitudes, and that negative signs and angles will be handled independently as we will see
