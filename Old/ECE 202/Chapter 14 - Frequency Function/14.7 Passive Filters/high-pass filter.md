A high pass filter is formed when the output of an RC circuit is taken off the resistor. The [[Transfer Function|transfer function]] is:
$$
H(\omega)=\frac{V_{o}}{V_{i}}=\frac{R}{R+\frac{1}{j\omega C}}
$$
![[Pasted image 20240408002233.png]]

Note that $H(0)=0$, $H(\infty)=0$. The figure shows the plot of $|H(\omega)|$ 

A **high-pass filter** is designed to pass all frequencies above its [[cutoff frequency]] $\omega_{c}$ 

A high-pass filter can also be formed when the output of an RL circuit is taken off the inductor
