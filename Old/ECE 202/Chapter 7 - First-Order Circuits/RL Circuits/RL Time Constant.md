The time constant for the RL circuit is:
$$
\tau=\frac{L}{R} 
$$
![[Figure 7.12.png]]

With $\tau$ having the unit of seconds the equation can be rewritten as:
$$
i(t)=I_{0}e^{-t/\tau} 
$$

The smaller the time constant $\tau$ of a circuit, the faster the rate of decay of the response.
