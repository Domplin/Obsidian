The **Complete Response** of an RL circuit is shown by the equation:
$$
i(t)=i(\infty)+[i(0)-i(\infty)]e^{-t/\tau}
$$
where $i(0)$ and $i(\infty)$ are the initial and final values respectively.

To find the [[RL Step Response]] requires 3 things
1) The initial inductor current $i(0)$ at $t=0$ 
2) the final inductor current $i(\infty)$ 
3) the time constant $\tau$
