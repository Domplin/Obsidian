**Average power transfer** can be written in terms of RMS values:
$$
P=\frac{1}{2}V_{m}I_{m}\cos(\theta_{v}-\theta_{i})=\frac{V_{m}}{\sqrt{ 2 }} \frac{I_{m}}{\sqrt{ 2 }}\cos(\theta_{v}-\theta_{i})
$$
$$
=V_{rms}I_{rms} \cos(\theta_{v}-\theta_{i})
$$

Average Power absorbed by a resistor can be written as:
$$
P=I_{rms}^2R=\frac{V_{rms}^2}{R}
$$
with current and voltage being multiplied and divided by, respectively
