The **Laplace transform** is an integral transformation of a function $f(t)$ from the time domain into the complex frequency domain giving $F(s)$ 

Given a function $f(t)$ it’s Laplace transform, denoted by $F(s)$ or $L[f(t)]$:
$$
L[f(t)]=F(s)=\int ^\infty_{0^-} f(t)e^{-st}dt
$$
where $s$ is a complex variable

The Laplace transform is known as the *one-sided* (or *unilateral*) Laplace transform. The *two-sided* (or *bilateral*) Laplace transform is given as:
$$
F(s) = \int ^{\infty}_{-\infty }f(t)e^{st} \, dt
$$
