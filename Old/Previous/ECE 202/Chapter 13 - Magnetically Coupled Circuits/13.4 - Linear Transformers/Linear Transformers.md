A **Transformer** is generally a four-terminal device comprising two (or more) magnetically coupled circuits

A transformer is said to be *linear* if the coils are wound on a magnetically linear material - a material for which the magnetic permeability is constant 

Such as:
- air
- plastic 
- Bakelite
- wood
Most materials are magnetically linear

They are sometimes called *air-core transformers* although not all of them are necessarily air-core
