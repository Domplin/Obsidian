The Admittance, **Y** is the reciprocal of impedance, measured in siemens (**S**).

The admittance **Y** of an element (or a circuit) is the ratio of the phasor current through it to the phasor voltage across it, or:
$$
Y=\frac{1}{Z}=\frac{I}{V}
$$
