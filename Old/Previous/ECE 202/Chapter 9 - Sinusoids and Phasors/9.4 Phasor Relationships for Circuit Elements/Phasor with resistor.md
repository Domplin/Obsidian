The current through the resistor R is $i = I_{m}\cos(\omega t+\phi)$. The voltage across it is given by [[Ohm's Law]] as:
$v=iR =RI_{m}\cos(\omega t+\phi)$

The phasor form of this voltage is:
$V=RI_{m}\angle\phi$

The phasor representation of the current is $I=I_{m}\angle\phi$ meaning:
$$
V=RI
$$
