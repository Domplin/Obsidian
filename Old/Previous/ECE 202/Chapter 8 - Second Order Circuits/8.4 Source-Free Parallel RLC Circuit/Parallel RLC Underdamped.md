When $\alpha<\omega_{0}$, $L<4R^2C$. In this case the roots are complex and may be expressed as 
$$
s_{1,2}=-\alpha\pm j\omega_{d}
$$
where
$$
\omega_{d}=\sqrt{ \omega_{0}^2-\alpha^2 }
$$

The response is 
$$
v(t)=e^{-\alpha t}(A_{1}\cos \omega_{d}t+A_{2}\sin \omega_{d}t)
$$
The constants $A_{1}$ and $A_{2}$ in each case can be determined from the initial conditions.
We need $v(0)$ and $\frac{dv(0)}{dt}$ :
$$
\frac{V_{0}}{R}+I_{0}+C \frac{dv(0)}{dt}=0
$$
or 
$$
\frac{dv(0)}{dt}=-\frac{V_{0}+RI_{0}}{RC}
$$
