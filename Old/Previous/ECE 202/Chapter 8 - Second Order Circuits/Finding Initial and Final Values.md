Keep in mind that capacitor voltage is always continuous that:
$$
v(0^+)=v(0^-)
$$
The inductor current is always continuous so that:
$$
i(0^+)=i(0^-)
$$


Assuming that the switching event takes place at $t=0$, $t=0^-$ denotes the time just before the switching event, and $t=0^+$ is the time just after switching.

In finding initial conditions focus on the variable that cannot change abruptly, capacitor voltage and inductor current.
