- Next to the storage cells, the sense amplifier is the most critical component in a memory chip.
	- Sense amplifiers offer speed and area improvements 


- The memory cell whose output is to amplified develops a difference output voltage between the $BL$ and $\overline{BL}$ lines.

- The signal - which can range from 20mV to 500mV depending on the memory type and cell design, will be amplified to the input terminals of the sense amplifier

- The sense amplifier in turn responds by providing a full swing (0 to VDD)
