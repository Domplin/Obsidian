- Assume the *source* and the *drain* are grounded an there is a positive voltage applied to the gate $V_{GS}>0$ 
- The positive gate voltage repels the free holes (*positively charged*) from the region under the gate (*the channel region*) 
- These holes are pushed downward into the substrate, leaving behind dopant ions (*depletion region*)

- At the same time the positive gate voltage attraction electrons from the $n^+$ source and drain regions, into the channel region
- When enough electrons accumulate near the surface of the substrate under the gate, an *n region is in effect created* - connecting the source and drain regions
- This induced $n^+$ region forms a channel for current flow from drain to source
