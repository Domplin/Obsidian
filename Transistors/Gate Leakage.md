- Gate leakage is the unwanted flow of current from the gate through the dielectric layer to the channel, substrate, or source/drain


- $t_{ox}$ has been scaling with each technology generation
- It has reached a point where $t_{ox}$ is so small that direct tunneling occurs ($t_{ox} < 2nm$) 
- Gate leakage = $f(t_{ox}, V_{G})$

![[Gate Leakage graph.png]]

**Using [[High-K Dielectric|high-k dielectric]] (thick) gate leakage can be suppressed**

$$
I_{gate} \propto e^{-t_{ox}/\lambda}

$$
