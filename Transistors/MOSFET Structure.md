- **Substrate**: The MOSFET transistor is fabricated on either a *p-type* or *n-type* substrate - which is a single silicon wafer 
- **Source / Drain**: Two heavily doped regions (*n or p type*) shown as the $n^+$ source and drain regions 
- **Gate Dielectric**: A thin layer of of silicon dioxide ($SiO_{2}$) of a thickness of $t_{ox} \approx 1nm \text{ to } 10nm$ 
	- It is an excellent electrical insulator
	- It is grown on the surface of the substrate covering the area between the source and drain regions 
- **Metal Contacts**: Metal is deposited on top of the oxide layer to form the gate contact 
	- Metal contacts are also made to the source, drain, and the substrate (or body)
