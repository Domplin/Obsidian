- Source / Drain form [[Diodes|diodes]] with bulk $\Rightarrow$ depletion regions are the dielectric 
- Dielectric width (and cap) varies with forward voltage on those diodes
- Geometry of capacitors are 3D
- Drain cap usually part of output cap 
- Source cap may or may not be neglected 

![[Parasitic Source and Drain Capacitance Diagram.png]]


$W$ = Gate width - $D$ = Diffusion length

$$
C_{SB}=(WD)C_{jbs}+(2W+2D)C_{jbssw}
$$
$$
C_{jbs}=C_{j}\left( 1+ \frac{V_{SB}}{\psi_{SW}} \right)
$$
$$
C_{jbssw}=C_{JSW}\left( 1+ \frac{V_{SB}}{\psi_{SW}} \right)^{-M_{JSW}}
$$
