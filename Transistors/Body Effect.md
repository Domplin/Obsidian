- A MOSFET is a four terminal device
- Source may not be at same potential bulk
- Effect of source-bulk bias is increased threshold voltage 
- $\gamma$ is body effect coefficient 
- $N_{A}$ is density of carries in doped substrate 
- $Ni$ is density of carries in intrinsic Si ($1.45*10^{10}cm^{-3}$ at 300k)

$$
V_{T}= V_{TO}+\gamma \lfloor \sqrt{ 2\Phi_{B}+|V_{SB}| }-\sqrt{ 2\Phi_{b} } \rfloor
$$
$$
\Phi_{b}=\frac{kT}{q}\ln\left( \frac{N_{A}}{N_{i}} \right)
$$
$$
\gamma=\frac{t_{ox}}{\epsilon_{ox}}\sqrt{ 2q \epsilon_{si}N_{A} }
$$
