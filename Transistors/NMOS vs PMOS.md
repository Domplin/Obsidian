![[NMOS vs PMOS.png]]

The structure of NMOS and PMOS transistors are similar except for the substrate, source, and drain regions are reversed polarity 
- **PMOS**
	- Source and Drain: $p^+$
	- Substrate: *n-type*
- **NMOS**
	- Source and Drain: $n^+$
	- Substrate: *p-type*

Due to them being the inverse of each other they are said to be complementary devices
