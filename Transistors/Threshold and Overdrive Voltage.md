![[Threshold and Overdrive Voltage.png]]

- The Value of $V_{GS}$ at which enough mobile electrons gather in the channel region to form a conducting channel is called the *Threshold voltage* $V_{th}$
- The $V_{th}$ for an *n-channel* MOSFET is positive and its negative for a *p-channel* MOSFET
- The *Overdrive voltage* is the quantity that determines the charge in the channel 


$$
V_{OV}= V_{GS}-V_{th}
$$
