- Doubling the drive strength of a gate will also double its capacitance 
	- 2 x gate (input) capacitance 
	- 2 x diffusion (output) capacitance 
- If the gate is driving a negligible load $t_{pd}$ wont change
-  The circuit driving this gate will be affected due to the gate capacitance increase.
