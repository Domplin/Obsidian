- For long-channel devices - the depletion layer width is small around junctions so $V_{T}$ does not change noticeably 
- For short-channel devices - as $V_{DS}$ is increased the depletion layer with continue to increase and help reduce $V_{T}$ 
- $V_{T}$ will continue to decrease as depletion layer thickness grows 
- If source and drain depletion regions merge - punch through happens
