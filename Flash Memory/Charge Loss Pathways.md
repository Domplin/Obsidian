**Charge Loss Mechanisms**
1) Thermionic emission
	1) Thermionic emission is the process in which electrons gain sufficient thermal energy to overcome an energy barrier and escape from a material. This mechanism becomes more significant at elevated temperatures (>$125^{\degree}C$). At room temperatures it is not the dominate charge loss mechanism
2) Trap assisted tunneling (TAT) 
	1) The most dominant mechanism - Electrons tunnel from the storage layer back to the silicon channel through the tunnel oxide via trap-assisted tunneling. This mechanism becomes increasingly dominant in aged flash cells.
3) De-trapping from oxide traps
	1) De-trapping causes rapid charge loss during the initial phase of data retention. Immediately after programming, some electrons become trapped in shallow interface or oxide defect states rather than in stable storage sites. During the early retention period, these electrons can quickly escape (de-trap) due to thermal energy
