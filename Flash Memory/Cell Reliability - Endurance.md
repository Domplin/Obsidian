The endurance of a memory module is defined as the minimum number of Program/Erase (PE) cycles that the module can withstand before leading to a failure

*Typical Endurance*:
- SLC: $10^5$ PE cycles
- MLC: $10^4$ PE cycles 
- TLC: $10^3$ PE cycles 


Repeated PE cycles introduces traps in the tunnel oxide


**How oxide traps are created**
- During program/erase operation hot holes are created due to impact ionization.
- Hot holes may get trapped in the oxide to do anode hole injection

**Effects of trap charge in the oxide**
- Tunneling efficiency reduces during both program and erase
