RTN is the fluctuation of cell $V_{TH}$ caused by charge-trapping/de-trapping at the channel/tunnel oxide interface traps, bulk tunnel oxide traps, and channel polysilicon grain boundary traps. This Fluctuation of $V_{TH}$ degrades state-width by increasing the inaccuracy of program-verify and read operations
